I am a Machine Learning Researcher at **SapientPriors**, where I work on **continual learning**: building AI agents that keep adapting after deployment in enterprise environments.

My research sits at the intersection of **computer vision**, **geometric deep learning** and **AI for science**, applied to complex physical and biological systems. Recent work includes:

- **Topology-aware 3D segmentation.** At Carnegie Mellon University (with Dr. Min Xu and Dr. Xingjian Li) I built [TopoFuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29717), a tri-planar fusion framework for cryo-electron tomography segmentation, accepted at **ECCV 2026**.
- **Learning for power systems.** At IIT Roorkee (with Dr. Parikshit Pareek) I designed a hybrid neural architecture whose differentiable power-balance and reserve-repair layers guarantee 100% feasibility of grid constraints. At Woosong University I built spatio-temporal GNNs for solar-irradiance forecasting and grid-stability control.
- **Signal intelligence.** With ISRO (Dr. Brahmjit Singh) I developed Bi-LSTM models for GNSS jamming detection, published at IEEE GCWCN 2025.

I graduated with a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from **NIT Kurukshetra** in 2026, where I was a founding member of the Anveshan Anusandhan Research Cell.

I also write about machine learning on my [blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}).
