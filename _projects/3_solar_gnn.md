**Woosong University, Daejeon** · Advisor: Dr. Surender Reddy Salkuti · May – Jul 2024 and May – Jul 2025

- Built a spatio-temporal graph neural network with **Topological Data Analysis** features for solar-irradiance forecasting.
- Fused graph convolutions with an **ARIMA**-based temporal model for predictive grid-stability control.
- Achieved a **72.8% reduction in voltage violations** in PV systems.
