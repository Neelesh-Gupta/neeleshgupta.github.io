**Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)** · Advisor: Dr. Brahmjit Singh · Jun 2024 – May 2025

- Simulated a **10,000-sample GNSS dataset** to systematically validate Bi-LSTM architectures for identifying and classifying radio-frequency jamming.
- Processed the time-series signals with overlapping sliding windows for feature extraction.
- Reached **over 99% classification accuracy** across dynamic 2 dB to 20 dB SNR environments.
